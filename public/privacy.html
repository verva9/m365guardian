Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

What we access

When you connect a Microsoft 365 tenant, we request read-only Microsoft Graph permissions: Conditional Access policies, directory role memberships, MFA registration details, guest invitation settings, and admin consent policy status. We never request or use permissions that allow modifying your tenant.

What we store

We store your tenant ID, a private access token used to view your own report, and the results of your most recent scans. We do not store individual user names, emails, or any content from your mailboxes, files, or Teams messages. Our checks only ever look at tenant-level configuration and aggregate MFA registration statistics.

How long we keep it

Scan data and connection records are automatically deleted after 90 days of inactivity. You can delete your data immediately at any time using the "Disconnect and delete my data" button on your report page.

Who can see your report

Your report is only accessible via a private link containing a randomly generated access token. Anyone with that link can view it, so treat it like a password. Do not share it publicly.
